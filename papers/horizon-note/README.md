# One trade per interval in a liquid market

A technical note: which forecast horizon the decision of a once-per-interval
agent in a liquid market actually uses, and what that implies for scoring
multi-step forecasts of foundation time-series models.

- `horizon-note.tex`, `horizon-note.bib`: source; compile with `tectonic horizon-note.tex`.
- `horizon-note.pdf`: the compiled note.

Three arguments kept apart: the exact sufficiency of the one-step law under
log utility (with Merton's hedging demand for other utilities and no smallness
claim); the empirical, unordered comparison of direct and iterated multi-step
forecasting; and the distinction between a proper score and a decision loss.
Revised after a referee's report, whose two counterexamples and first-step
identity are included with credit.
