The learned model: laplace at worst

skaters.torch.learned trained on half of FRED, by family scored on the other half k = 1 issue #297

Two forecasters run on every series and an exact mixture sits on top. The floor is laplace, the reference implementation, frozen. The challenger is the torch twin of laplace: the same pool of candidate chains, trunk, lattice projection, tail splice and parade, initialised at laplace's values, with two things that learn. The continuous hyperparameters of the transforms and the trunk (smoothing rates, mean-reversion and GARCH rates, autoregressive ridge and decay, power and Yeo–Johnson exponents, the trunk's learning rate, forgetting and depth penalty) are nn.Parameters. And a small gate reads the parade's z, the trunk's prequential log weights and two running descriptors, and adds an offset to those log weights at the combine step, so it tilts which candidates carry the forecast. The gate starts at zero.

The mixture weights the two experts by their undiscounted cumulative log-likelihood, the Aggregating Algorithm under log loss, one instance per phase at each horizon. That is the guarantee: on any series the mixture's cumulative log loss exceeds laplace's by at most h·log(1/π) nats, with π laplace's prior share (0.5 in the run on this page). Nothing training does to the challenger can break it. The learned part can only re-tune laplace's own knobs and re-weight laplace's own candidates; if an edge cannot be expressed that way, this model cannot find it.

Training and the split

FRED is split by family (fred_universe.family: a curve of maturities, an FX panel, a release's components land on one side), so the validation half shares no family with the training half. Training is prequential: the forecast for yt is issued before yt arrives, scored against it, and the loss of a 64-step window, the challenger's own log score, is backpropagated at the window boundary; Adam steps, every bounded parameter is clamped back into its valid region, and the state is detached. The mixture is not trained. The in-sample signal over the training pass, the challenger's mean log score next to the frozen laplace's on the same steps:

Validation

Scored through the canonical pipeline on the validation half, paired against laplace on identical series and windows. Win, draw and loss are decided one series at a time by a Diebold–Mariano test on the per-step log-score differential over the 64-point test window (floor −20, Newey–West standard error, ±1.96 SE; a draw says the window cannot separate the two at the 5% level, not that they are equal). The densities are the model's own; there is no quantile reconstruction on this page.

Median per-series Δ log-likelihood in nats (negative is worse than Laplace); CRPS ratio to Laplace (above 1 is worse); central-90% coverage (0.90 target).

On daily econ the mixture has more losses than wins, 52 to 88 of 400, at a median of -0.000 nats; the trained expert alone is 45 to 77 at -0.008. On weekly the mixture has more wins than losses, 96 to 17 of 400, at a median of +0.009 nats; the trained expert alone is 60 to 20 at +0.011. On monthly the mixture has more wins than losses, 88 to 33 of 400, at a median of +0.005 nats; the trained expert alone is 96 to 16 at +0.037.

The mixture and the expert differ where their numbers differ. The mixture's weights are undiscounted over the whole history, as the bound requires, so where the challenger was weaker early in a series it never fully regains weight; the expert alone carries no such drag and no such guarantee.

Star plot

The mixture standalone against Laplace on the six regime axes of the challengers radar: the share of series where its mean log score is the higher, ties split, divided by one half, so an even split sits on the dashed 1.0 ring. Axes without 30 shared series are left empty.

What is not here

  • Daily economic series are laplace's home and the learned model does not improve them; training is now confined to weekly and monthly.
  • Only k = 1 has been trained. The multi-step case, where the multiscale machinery lives, is next.
  • GIFT-Eval, the clean out-of-domain test, has not been run on this model.
