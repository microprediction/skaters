The sandwich

Run any model in laplace's coordinates, map back exactly. Forecasters converge to laplace plus whatever they see that it missed; detectors that want stationary input improve, template matchers do not.

Featured sandwich of the day: Prophet. Raw, it trails laplace by 0.76 nats median across 921 series (4.6 family-weighted). Sandwiched, 0.02. That is 97% of its density gap closed without retraining anything, calendar machinery intact, via pip install prophet-laplace.

laplace defines a causal bijection on paths: the Rosenblatt transform of each observation under the predictive issued for it. Feed the resulting z stream to any model, map its output back through the exact Jacobian, and the model operates on a stationarised, unit-scale stream instead of raw data. No retraining, no approximation in the accounting. The skill has the recipe; prophet-laplace is the drop-in package for Prophet.

PYMC-Forecast: current canonical result

Under the canonical protocol, laplace@PyMC has negative median paired Δ log-likelihood against plain laplace on each economic stratum: −0.0522 daily (500 series), −0.0124 weekly (499), and −0.0153 monthly (471). Positive would mean better than laplace. These are the committed canonical summary results also reported on the sidecar page. The general claim that the PyMC sandwich edges laplace is withdrawn.

The July 226-series study remains available on the historical bout page. It reported its block-refit sand arm ahead on 65% of series, with +0.04 nats median. Its separate frozen solo arm, fit once before the 150-step test window, was reported with a +0.005-nat median gap. These are distinct arms, not two summaries of one fit. The canonical adapter refits and emits predictive distributions through the common density reconstruction and scoring protocol; the old results therefore do not establish a canonical gain or that batch-refit staleness is negligible. The historical pooling contrast is retained on the bout page and was not rerun here.

The older seasonal study (benchmarks/pymc_arms_study.py) used the frozen z-space fit and reported positive sign-comparison rates of 55% weekly, 64% monthly, and 84% on M4-Hourly, with median lifts +0.009, +0.017, and +0.063 nats. Those are historical results under a different protocol; the canonical weekly and monthly medians above are negative. The canonical rerun did not cover M4-Hourly, so it neither confirms nor retracts that older hourly result. Sign-comparison percentages must not be confused with the canonical HAC/DM win/draw/loss rule.1

The detector rows locate the boundary: the sandwich lifts methods whose theorems want a stationary, calibrated input, does little for weak structural methods, and hurts template matchers. It transfers the forecaster's competence, in both directions.
