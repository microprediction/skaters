The paper

Transforms All the Way Down — Automatic Online Distributional Forecasting by Conjugation. P. Cotton.

Every prediction is a full predictive distribution, assembled by composition: invertible transforms chain together above a single distributional leaf, and ensembles combine such chains. Because the leaf fits its shape by optimising a proper scoring rule, its objective is a choice — fit the model by likelihood, then conform the predictive tail to a downstream score such as CRPS. The library is written twice, in pure Python and in zero-dependency JavaScript that agrees to 1e-6, so the same model runs on a server or in a browser.

Companion paper — the conformal information gap

An Empirical Study of the Conformal Information Gap. P. Cotton.

The library serves as the measurement instrument for a study of what residual pooling costs. Under logarithmic loss the irreducible cost of a retained representation is a conditional mutual information, the information gap. A nested ladder of skaters transforms prices each representation family prequentially on 701 FRED series: conditional scale is the largest and most reliable component of the gain, and scale-normalized empirical pooling beats raw pooling on 87 percent of series under CRPS. The experiment scripts live in this repository's benchmarks directory. Preprint on SSRN (2026).
