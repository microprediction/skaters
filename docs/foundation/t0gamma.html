t0-gamma vs Laplace

The Forecasting Company · t0-gamma 256M Apache-2.0 zero-shot one-step change series

t0-gamma is The Forecasting Company's third public t0 checkpoint, released 13 October 2026: a 256M-parameter decoder-style patch transformer that predicts 21 quantile levels natively and ranks first among single zero-shot models on fev-bench and TIME. Run zero-shot on CPU with a fixed 128-length context (four 32-point patches) through the direct predict path, with the nonnegativity clip, integer snapping and sign-flip averaging switched off and no cross-series inference. The headline row is all 21 native levels; the nine-decile subset and the vendor's sign-flip averaging are tabled below.

Resources: GitHub · Model card · Paper (arXiv 2609.24559) · Release post

This page measures one thing: how sharp and how well calibrated t0-gamma's univariate predictive density is, one step ahead on a change series, scored against Laplace on identical windows. It leaves out the covariates, the multivariate inputs, the long horizons and the native quantile or path outputs the model was built for, and its log score passes through a reconstruction of the model's quantiles (the table note says which). A model can trail here and be the better tool for its own job.

Live snapshot  Derived from the week-long round-robin study; counts grow as coverage deepens. Everything scores against Laplace on the identical series and windows.

Standalone

The model's own predictive, run zero-shot, scored per series against Laplace. Win, draw and loss are decided one series at a time. Each series has a rolling test window of 64 one-step-ahead points, and both forecasters are scored on the same points. For each point take the difference of log predictive densities, dt = log pmodel(yt) − log pLaplace(yt), with each log density floored at −20 first. The series' mean d̄ is tested against zero by a Diebold–Mariano test: the standard error is Newey–West with a Bartlett kernel and lag round(n1/3). The model wins the series if d̄ > 1.96·SE, loses it if d̄ < −1.96·SE, and otherwise the series is a draw; a series with fewer than eight finite differences is a draw. A draw says the 64 points cannot separate the two forecasters at the 5% level. It does not say they are equal. With a window this short both win and loss counts understate the true rates, and the median ΔLL is the better measure of size. Counts are over the series both arms cover.

Protocol

Fixed 128-length context, rolling one-step test window, no fitting, each model in its own environment. Strata split the cached FRED universe and the M4-hourly set by frequency and regime. Full method on the sidecar pattern page and in the methodology.

Architecture and methodology

t0 is a decoder-style patch transformer built for forecasting with covariates and multivariate context, with a 32-step patch and a 1,024-step native horizon; none of the covariate machinery is used here. The paper (arXiv 2609.24559) documents the alpha and beta checkpoints; gamma's training recipe is described only in the release post. On its native 21 levels it is within 0.1 nats of Laplace on daily econ, level on price and weekly, and ahead on monthly and M4-hourly, with coverage 0.89 to 0.91. The vendor's sign-flip averaging adds about 0.02 nats at twice the runtime. Pretraining contamination is unchecked.
